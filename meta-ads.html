What ads really cost, and what to expect.

Where the Money Goes

Running ads has three costs

A lot of people get one number and never see what's inside it. Here's the breakdown you should always get.

Paid to Meta
Ad spend

The money Meta charges to show your ad. It's billed straight to your card, in your own ad account, so you see every dollar. None of it goes to me.

The creative
The ads themselves

The videos and photos people actually see. This is the biggest reason an ad works or doesn't. I shoot and edit these for your business, not from a template.

The work
Running the ads

Setting up campaigns, picking who sees them, testing, and moving money to what's working. I handle this for a monthly fee, quoted on our call.

If someone quotes you one number for "ads," ask how much of it actually goes to Meta.

Month by Month

Ads take a few months to work

This is the part most people don't hear upfront. The first month is not the payoff month. Here's how it actually goes.

Month 1Gathering data

Meta is learning who your customers are

We launch a few different ads and let them run. Results bounce around, and that's normal. Every view, click, and message is data. Shutting ads off in week one is the most common mistake.

Month 2More data, first lessons

Patterns start to show up

With more data, we can see which ads people stop for and which ones they scroll past. We cut what isn't working, make new versions of what is, and keep learning.

Month 3Seeing what works

You know what's working

By now there's a clear picture of which ads, offers, and audiences bring in customers. More budget goes behind the winners. This is the fair point to judge whether ads are paying off.

Straight Talk

Things nobody tells you

You deserve to know this before you spend a dollar, whoever you end up working with.

Your ad account should be yours

Ads should run in your own Meta account, on your own page. If they run in someone else's account and you leave, all that learning leaves with them. I set everything up in yours.

Nobody can guarantee results

Be careful with anyone who promises a set number of leads or a dollar return. Good ads are tested and improved. They aren't guaranteed.

The video matters more than the targeting

Meta is very good at finding the right people now. What it can't do is make a boring ad interesting. The ad itself is where most of the results come from.

Boosting a post isn't the same as running ads

The Boost button is fine for a quick push. Real campaigns let you test different ads, track what happens after the click, and spend smarter.

You should get a simple report

What you spent, what it brought in, and what's changing next. In plain English, not a 20 page export.

Ads don't replace your page

People check your Instagram before they buy. Ads bring them in, and your regular content is what convinces them. The two work together.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need a big budget to start?

No. $200 to $500 a month can work for a local spot with a clear offer, like a new menu item, an event, or a first-time discount. Start small, learn what works, then grow it.

How long until I see results?

You'll see views and clicks right away. Knowing what actually brings in customers takes about three months. Month one is data, month two is more data and first lessons, and by month three you know what works.

Facebook or Instagram?

Both. They run from the same place, Meta Ads Manager, and one campaign can show on both. Meta puts your ad wherever your customers are spending time, and we can adjust from there.

Can't I just boost my posts?

You can, and it's fine for a quick push. But boosting skips most of what makes ads work: testing different versions, choosing a clear goal like messages or bookings, and tracking what happens after someone taps.

What do you need from me?

Access to your Facebook page and Instagram, a card on file in your Meta ad account for ad spend, and something worth promoting. I'll walk you through the setup on a call. It takes about 15 minutes.

Do you need my card or bank info?

No. Your card goes into your own Meta ad account, and Meta bills you directly. I never see it.

You give my business partner access from your Meta business settings. That lets me run campaigns without touching billing. You can set a monthly spending cap so ads can never go over your budget, and you can remove my access anytime with one click.

What does it cost to have you run my ads?

It depends on your budget and how many ads we're running. I'll give you a real number on our call. Your ad spend is always separate and always goes straight to Meta.

Are ads right for my business?

Ads work best when you have a clear offer and can handle more customers. Restaurants with a special or event, agents with a listing, salons and med spas with open booking times, and events selling tickets are all great fits.

If you're not sure, that's what the call is for. If ads aren't the right move yet, I'll tell you.
